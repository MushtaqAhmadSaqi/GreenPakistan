- **ReGongches Logo & Mark (`assets/img/logo.svg`, `logo-mark.svg`, `favicon.svg`):** Custom inline SVG artwork created specifically for the ReGongches project brief and business plan (*Mushtaq Ahmad, SP25-BAI-047*), combining three Karakoram/Himalayan mountain peaks (`#1F3A5F`), a flowing heritage maroon textile curve (`#7A1F2B`), and an antique gold lozenge embroidery motif (`#B8913A`).

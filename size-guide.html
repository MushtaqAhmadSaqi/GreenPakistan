Precision Protocol

How to Measure for Authentic Mountain Fit

Traditional Gilgit-Baltistan garments are tailored with generous room for layered mountain woollens. Follow these measuring tips:

1. Head Circumference (Pakol)

Wrap a soft measuring tape horizontally around your forehead, roughly 1 cm above your eyebrows and ears. Pakols feature a rolled brim that naturally stretches 1–2 cm to mould to your crown.

2. Chest Measurement (Robes & Waistcoats)

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level under your arms. Stand relaxed. If you plan to wear a thick kurta or wool sweater underneath, add 2–3 cm of ease.

3. Garment Length & Sleeve

For Shuqa robes, length is measured from the base of the back collar down to mid-calf. Sleeve length is measured from the centre-back collar across the shoulder to the wrist bone.

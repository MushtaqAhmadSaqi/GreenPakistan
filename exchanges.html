Simple Workflow

How to Exchange in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Message Our Gilgit Desk on WhatsApp: Send a quick message to +92 300 123 4567 with your Order Reference (e.g. RG-2026-XXXX) and the new size or piece you require.
  2. Repack in Original Presentation Box: Ensure the garment is unworn, unwashed, and neatly folded with the original Story Card and tags inside the breathable pouch.
  3. Hand Over to Courier at Your Door: We schedule a reverse pickup via TCS or Leopards Courier. The rider collects your parcel from your home or office, and your replacement piece is dispatched immediately upon tracking confirmation.
Eligibility Guidelines

Exchange Conditions & Requirements

To qualify for a smooth exchange, please ensure:

  • Unworn & Unwashed: Garments must not show perfume scents, body odors, makeup marks, or washing detergent residue.
  • Original Tags & Card Intact: The numbered Gilgit-Baltistan Story Card and woven label must remain attached.
  • Initiated within 14 Days: Your WhatsApp exchange request must be sent within 14 calendar days of confirmed courier delivery.

Courier Safeguard

Damaged or Defective in Transit Fast-Track

Mountain roads can be rugged. If your package arrives damaged by courier mishandling:

  • Take a quick photo or video of the courier flyer and damaged item.
  • Send it to our WhatsApp concierge within 48 hours of delivery.
  • We will immediately dispatch a fresh replacement piece from our studio at zero cost, without waiting for the courier investigation.
Answers

Exchange & Return FAQs

How long does a replacement shipment take?

Once reverse courier tracking is confirmed, your replacement size is dispatched within 24–48 hours from Gilgit, reaching major cities in 2–4 business days.

Can I exchange for a different item or color?

Yes. You can exchange for any piece in our archive. If the new item is priced differently, our concierge will coordinate a simple top-up or refund via Bank Transfer or JazzCash.

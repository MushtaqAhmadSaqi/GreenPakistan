Craft Process

From Mountain Wool to Wearable Heritage

01

Shearing & Drop-Spindle Spinning

High-altitude fleece is washed in cold mountain water, carded by hand, and spun into lofty two-ply yarn.

02

Treadle & Pit-Loom Weaving

Weavers throw wooden shuttles across narrow and wide looms to create dense patti strips and diamond twill shawls.

03

Counted-Thread Silk Embroidery

Artisans count individual warp threads to stitch symmetrical geometric lozenge motifs without printed guides.

04

Quality Check & Origin Card

Every garment is measured in cm and inches, inspected for stitch tension, and paired with its printed Origin Card.

    Highland Wool & Silk Embroidery Care Protocol

    1. Air in Shade

    Natural sheep and yak wool fibres are self-refreshing. Hang in a shaded, breezy room after wear rather than washing frequently.

    2. Cold Soak Only

    Use cold water and pH-neutral wool detergent. Never wring, twist, or machine-wash, as heat + agitation felts natural wool.

    3. Dry Flat & Cedar Store

    Roll in a towel to press out moisture, dry flat in the shade, and store folded in a breathable cotton pouch with Himalayan cedar wood.

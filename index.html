01 · Executive Summary & Brand Identity

Brand Promise: Authentic mountain clothing with a story of origin.

E-Commerce Type: Business-to-Consumer (B2C).

Core Offer: Traditional clothing and textile products from Gilgit-Baltistan, supported by origin information and cultural storytelling.

Domain: regongches.store · Hosting: HosterSale · Platform: WordPress + WooCommerce

Logo Elements: Mountain peaks (regional origin), flowing fabric (textile elegance), geometric motif (embroidery craftsmanship).

Colours: Mountain Blue (trust, stability), Heritage Maroon (cultural richness), Antique Gold (quality, craftsmanship).

Tagline: "Threads of Mountain Heritage" — connecting clothing with place and tradition.

Cultural accuracy note: Spelling and linguistic meaning of "ReGongches" should be confirmed with a native speaker or regional language expert before public launch.

02 · Business Objectives

Vision: To become a trusted digital destination for authentic traditional clothing from Gilgit-Baltistan, making the region's textile heritage accessible across Pakistan and beyond.

Mission: To preserve and promote the clothing heritage of Gilgit-Baltistan by connecting local artisans with customers through authentic products, transparent sourcing, cultural storytelling and fair business practices.

Strategic Objectives:

  • Launch a mobile-friendly B2C store with a focused product catalogue.
  • Build direct relationships with artisans, tailoring units and regional suppliers.
  • Develop professional photography, size guidance and product storytelling.
  • Reach customers across Pakistan through digital marketing and tracked delivery.
  • Prepare the platform for future verified artisan marketplace participation.

04 · Market Strategy

Primary Audience: Young adults interested in cultural fashion, people from Gilgit-Baltistan living elsewhere, event customers, domestic tourists and gift buyers.

Secondary Audience: Pakistani diaspora, universities, cultural societies, hotels and corporate gift buyers.

Positioning: A trusted online destination for authentic traditional clothing and textile heritage from Gilgit-Baltistan.

Promotion Channels: Short videos, artisan stories, cultural-event partnerships, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp Business.

Launch Campaign — Threads of the North: A 30-day digital launch campaign featuring one product, craft process, artisan story or regional tradition per day.

05 · SMART Marketing Goal

Six-month goal (1 Jan 2027 – 30 Jun 2027): Complete 300 customer orders, gain 2,500 relevant social-media followers, achieve a 15% repeat-purchase rate and maintain at least 85% positive customer feedback.

  • Specific: Increase orders, audience, repeat purchasing and satisfaction.
  • Measurable: 300 orders · 2,500 followers · 15% repeat purchase · 85% positive feedback.
  • Achievable: Use focused inventory, cultural content, event partnerships and referrals.
  • Relevant: Supports brand growth, regional craft visibility and sustainable operations.
  • Time-Bound: 1 January 2027 to 30 June 2027.

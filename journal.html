Apparel Measurement Standard

ReGongches Size & Fit Guide (cm & inches)

Head Circumference: 56 cm (22.0 in) → Recommended ReGongches Size: Medium (M — 56 cm)

How to measure your head for a Gilgiti or Hunza Cap: Wrap a flexible tape measure around your head 1.5 cm (0.6 in) above your ears and across the middle of your forehead. Hold comfortably snug.

Label SizeHead Circumference (cm)Head Circumference (inches)Fit Recommendation
Small (S)54 cm21.2 inSnug crown; youths or smaller adult head
Medium (M)56 cm22.0 inMost common standard adult fit
Large (L)58 cm22.8 inRelaxed fit or thicker hair
Extra Large (XL)60 cm23.6 inGenerous crown circumference

How to measure for a Pattu Waistcoat or Jacket: Measure around the fullest part of your chest over a light kameez or shirt. Our waistcoats include a 4 cm (1.5 in) comfort ease.

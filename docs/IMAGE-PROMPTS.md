# ReGongches Image Generation Prompts Catalogue

**Standard Photography Style Guide:**  
- **Lighting & Setting:** Warm off-white linen backdrop, soft natural daylight entering from upper left, gentle realistic cast shadow, neutral color temperature (5200K).
- **Camera & Lens:** 85mm portrait/product prime lens at f/5.6, 4:5 vertical composition (1600×2000), generous margins, sharp texture focus.
- **Negative Prompt:** Indian paisley or Persian rug patterns mixed in, plastic or synthetic sheen, distorted hands, extra fingers, garbled lettering, oversaturated colours, fantasy costume look, text, logos, watermarks.
- **Lighting and colour grading are held identical across all 25 products for photoshoot unity.**

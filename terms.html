Living Heritage

Handmade Craft Variations Are Hallmarks of Authenticity

ReGongches garments and pattu textiles are crafted on traditional wooden pit-looms and frame-looms in mountain villages, not by automated industrial robotics.

Natural Fibre & Hand-Loom Signature

Subtle slubs in hand-spun wool yarn, minute variations in natural plant dyes (walnut bark, madder root, onion skin), and unique embroidery stitch tensions are the authentic signatures of human craftsmanship, never industrial flaws.

Currency & Billing

Pricing & Pakistani Rupee Settlement

All prices listed on ReGongches are officially settled in Pakistani Rupees (PKR):

  • Display Conversions (USD, GBP, AED): Currency conversions displayed on the website are approximate estimates based on indicative exchange rates. Final billing and invoice totals are processed strictly in PKR.
  • Price Inclusivity: Prices listed for catalogue pieces include artisan guild payments and regional sourcing taxes. Courier fees are calculated based on your total order value (free over PKR 8,000).
Delivery Ceiling

Cash on Delivery Limit (PKR 25,000)

In strict accordance with Pakistani courier logistics regulations and transit insurance guidelines:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is available nationwide up to a maximum total of PKR 25,000 per order.
  • Orders exceeding PKR 25,000 require advance settlement via Direct Bank Transfer (Meezan Bank IBAN / Raast) or JazzCash/EasyPaisa mobile wallet prior to courier dispatch.
Custom Commissions

Bespoke Tailoring & Wedding Commissions

For custom-fitted pieces (such as custom Shuqa robes, bridal ensembles, or custom waistcoats):

  • 50% Advance Deposit: Bespoke commissions require a 50% non-refundable advance payment to fund wool yarn preparation and weaver loom setup.
  • Artisan Lead Time: Hand-looming and custom embroidery require 5–12 business days depending on motif complexity.
  • Complimentary Alterations: Bespoke garments are non-refundable once cut; however, our studio provides free alteration adjustments if finished measurements deviate from agreed specifications.

Highland Wisdom

The Three Golden Rules of Mountain Wool

Centuries of mountain textile culture in Hunza, Gilgit, and Baltistan teach us that natural sheep and yak wool does not behave like synthetic fast fashion.

1. Air in Shade, Don’t Wash

Natural sheep wool contains microscopic scales coated in trace lanolin that naturally repel odor and moisture. Hang your piece outdoors in a cool, shaded breeze after wearing; it will self-refresh without water.

2. Never Wring, Never Agitate

Hot water and machine spinning cause wool scales to interlock permanently—this is called accidental felting, which shrinks garments. Cold water soaking and gentle towel-pressing are essential.

3. Natural Cedar, Never Chemical Mothballs

Toxic paradichlorobenzene mothballs leave lingering foul chemical odors and dry out natural wool lanolin. Genuine Himalayan cedar wood naturally deters pests while keeping wool fragrant.

Step-by-Step Cleaning

How to Hand-Wash Hand-Loomed Pattu & Pakols

Wash only when strictly necessary—usually once at the end of the winter season before summer storage.

  1. Fill Basin with Cold Water: Use water below 20°C (cool to the touch). Add a few drops of pH-neutral liquid wool wash or mild organic Castile soap. Avoid biological detergents containing harsh proteases.
  2. Gentle Submersion: Submerge your shawl or cap and gently press down. Allow it to soak for 10–15 minutes without rubbing, wringing, or scrubbing.
  3. Cold Rinse: Drain soapy water and refill with clean cold water. Gently press the garment to rinse out soap residues until clear.
  4. Towel Roll Extraction: Lay a clean dry bath towel flat, place your wet garment on top, and roll it up like a cylinder. Gently press down on the roll with your palms to extract excess moisture.
  5. Reshape & Dry Flat: Unroll and place on a dry towel or mesh drying rack in an airy, shaded spot away from direct heat or harsh sun. Never hang a wet wool garment on a coat hanger, as the weight of water will stretch the shoulders.
Spot Cleaning Guide

Tea, Oil & Mud Removal Protocol

Spills happen during chai gatherings and weddings. Act quickly with these cold-treatment methods:

Spill Type Immediate Action Safe Cleaning Agent What to Avoid
Chai / Black Tea Blot immediately with a clean dry cotton cloth. Do NOT rub into the fibres. Dab with a mixture of cold water and white vinegar (1:1 ratio). Rinse with cold water dab. Hot water or alkaline soap, which sets tannin stains permanently.
Dry Mountain Mud / Dust Let the mud dry completely first! Gently brush off dry crust with a soft horsehair clothes brush along the grain of the weave. Wetting wet mud, which pushes silt deep into wool yarn twists.
Grease / Food Oils Sprinkle cornstarch or talcum powder directly on the stain; leave for 2 hours to absorb. Brush off powder, then gently dab with a drop of mild wool wash on a cold damp cloth. Aggressive rubbing, bleach, or chlorine stain pens.
Summer Storage

Off-Season Preservation & Cedar Wood

When high-altitude winter passes, prepare your pieces for warm weather rest. Moths are attracted to body oils and food residues left on unwashed collars, so ensure items are clean and completely dry before storing.

  • Breathable Cotton Bags: Store shawls and robes folded in 100% breathable unbleached cotton bags (like the ReGongches provenance pouch supplied with your order). Avoid airtight plastic bins or poly bags, which trap humidity and cause mildew.
  • Himalayan Cedar Shavings: Place natural cedar blocks or dried lavender sachets inside the bag. Sand cedar blocks lightly once a year to rejuvenate their natural aromatic oils.
  • Mid-Summer Inspection: Take your pieces out once every mid-summer, give them a gentle shake, and let them hang in dry outdoor shade for an hour before replacing them in storage.
Decision Matrix

Dry Cleaning vs. Hand Washing

When is professional dry cleaning recommended, and when is home care best?

Garment Type Recommended Method Notes & Safeguards
Hunza Pattu Shawls & Wraps Cold Hand Wash Natural wool thrives with cold water and gentle flat drying; dry cleaning solvents can strip natural softness.
Gilgiti Pakol Caps Spot Clean & Brush Submerging can soften the stiffened brim fold. Brush frequently and spot-clean the forehead sweatband.
Embroidered Waistcoats & Jackets Delicate Dry Clean Only Structured interior canvas, tailored lapels, and silk thread embroidery require professional pressing without moisture.
Ghizer Shuqa Woollen Robes Delicate Dry Clean Only Due to full length and tailored shoulder construction, professional eco-friendly dry cleaning is safest.
Wrinkle Removal

Steaming & Ironing Protocol

Never apply a hot, dry electric iron directly to pattu wool!

Direct dry heat will scorch wool fibres and create an irreversible shiny glazed surface.

  • Garment Steamer (Preferred): Hold a handheld steamer 2–3 inches away from the hanging garment. Allow the steam to penetrate the weave; wool will naturally relax and drop its wrinkles.
  • Pressing Cloth Technique: If an iron is necessary, set it to the lowest "Wool" setting with steam. Always place a clean damp white cotton towel between the iron soleplate and your wool garment. Gently press down and lift—never slide the iron aggressively.

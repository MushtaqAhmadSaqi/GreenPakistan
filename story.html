Our point of view

Made by hands.
Preserved through stories.

ReGongches is a bridge between the makers of Gilgit-Baltistan and the people who value an object with a living history.

The work behind the weave

Heritage is not a trend.

Across the high valleys, cloth is a record of season, family and place. We work with weavers, embroiderers and tailors through long-term relationships, fair pricing and full permission to share their work.

Each piece is documented with its region, material, technique and maker collective so that the value travels with the textile.

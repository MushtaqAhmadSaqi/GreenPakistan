      3-Step Highland Wool & Silk Embroidery Care Protocol

      1. Air in Shade

      Natural sheep and yak wool fibres are self-refreshing. Hang in a shaded, breezy room after wear rather than washing frequently.

      2. Cold Soak Only

      Use cold water and pH-neutral wool wash. Never wring, twist, or machine-wash, as heat and agitation felt natural wool.

      3. Dry Flat & Cedar Store

      Press moisture in a towel, dry flat in shade, and store folded in a breathable cotton bag with natural Himalayan cedar wood.

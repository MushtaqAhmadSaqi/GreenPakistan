Provenance Pipeline

How Your Mountain Heritage Piece Travels

01. Order Logged

Your order specification, selected garment sizes, and colour options are recorded in our Jutial studio log.

02. Artisan Guild Audit

The valley weaver, fulling tension, and silk embroidery are inspected under natural daylight in Gilgit.

03. Packed & Sealed

The piece is folded with organic cedar shavings, sealed in breathable cotton wrap, and issued its numbered Story Card.

04. Courier In-Transit

Handed to our courier partners (TCS, Leopards, Trax) for express air/road transit down the Karakoram Highway.

05. Doorstep Delivery

Delivered to your doorstep. Cash on Delivery (COD) collected with 14-day exchange guarantee intact.

Common Questions

Tracking & Delivery Help

What if my Order Reference doesn't show up?

If you ordered from a different browser or cleared your device storage, simply click "Message Dispatch Desk" or tap our floating WhatsApp button. Send us your name and delivery city, and our Gilgit studio will immediately share your current dispatch status and courier tracking link.

What is the average transit time to my city?

Orders to Gilgit and Skardu arrive in 1–2 business days. Major hubs (Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Peshawar, Karachi) arrive in 2–4 business days via express courier. See our full Shipping & Delivery Policy for all 13 regional cities.

Can I change my delivery address after dispatch?

Yes. As long as the package has not reached out-for-delivery status in your city, our Gilgit dispatch desk can contact the courier liaison to reroute your package.

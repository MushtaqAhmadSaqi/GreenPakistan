Client-Side Architecture

What We Store in Your Browser (localStorage)

To provide a fast, private, zero-build shopping experience, our application stores essential storefront state solely on your own device using the standard web localStorage API. Here is the complete list of keys we use:

Storage Key Purpose & Data Stored Duration
regongches_cart Selected items, quantities, size choices, and gift wrap preferences. Persistent until checkout or manual removal
regongches_wishlist Array of product IDs you explicitly saved to your wishlist. Persistent
regongches_orders Order reference, city, and total for recent checkouts on this device, used by the Order Tracker. Persistent on device
regongches_currency Your preferred display currency (PKR, USD, GBP, or AED). Persistent
regongches_ann_dismissed Remembers if you clicked dismiss on the top announcement bar so it doesn't interrupt your reading. Session / 7 Days
Direct Messaging

WhatsApp Order Communication & Privacy

When you place an order or contact our concierge, our system prepares a structured message in your WhatsApp app:

  • Your Direct Control: You have full visibility of the text before tapping "Send". No background messaging occurs without your explicit action.
  • End-to-End Encryption: Once sent, your communication with our Gilgit studio is protected by WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption.
  • Zero Contact Resale: Your phone number is used exclusively for dispatch updates, courier consignment numbers, and sizing verification. We never send automated spam broadcasts.
Financial Discretion

Payment & Banking Discretion

We operate exclusively through verified, bank-standard Pakistani channels:

  • No Stored Card Data: We never collect, process, or store credit or debit card numbers, PINs, or CVV codes.
  • Direct Bank Transfers: Transfers are conducted securely through your own verified banking app (Meezan Bank IBAN / Raast).
  • Mobile Wallets: JazzCash and EasyPaisa payments are sent directly from your verified mobile wallet to our studio account.

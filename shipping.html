Shipping Rates & Free Delivery Threshold

Payment Protection

Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules & Limits

Cash on Delivery is available nationwide up to PKR 25,000.

Orders exceeding PKR 25,000 require payment via Direct Bank Transfer (IBAN/Raast) or JazzCash/EasyPaisa due to courier insurance ceilings.

To protect both our craftspeople and customers, Pakistani courier insurance limits Cash on Delivery parcel collections to PKR 25,000 per shipment. If your order exceeds this amount:

  • You may settle the full balance in advance via Bank Transfer (Meezan Bank IBAN) or mobile wallet.
  • Or you can place a 50% advance deposit via WhatsApp concierge, with the remaining balance split across safe COD milestones.

Questions & Answers

Shipping & Delivery FAQs

Can I open and inspect the parcel before paying COD?

National courier policy requires the payment to be collected prior to handing over the package seal. However, ReGongches provides a comprehensive 14-Day Easy Exchange Policy. If the piece does not match your expectations or needs a different size, our studio coordinates an immediate exchange.

Do you ship internationally (US, UK, UAE, Canada)?

Yes. International orders are handled individually via DHL Express. Reach our Gilgit concierge on WhatsApp (+92 300 123 4567) with your overseas delivery address for a calculated international air freight quotation.
